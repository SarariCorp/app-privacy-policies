Reusable App Policy Template

Copy /apps/example-app/ to /apps/<your-app-slug>/ to keep each app folder self-contained.

Each app folder should include:

  • index.html (the policy page)
  • logo.svg or logo.png (replaceable app logo asset)

All bracketed fields in the example policy are required placeholders and must be customized before publication.
