Reusable app folder template

Use this structure for each app folder:

  • apps/<app-slug>/index.html — app-specific privacy policy page
  • apps/<app-slug>/logo.svg (or logo.png) — app logo asset or clearly marked placeholder

Fast start: copy /apps/example-app/ to /apps/<your-app-slug>/, then replace every bracketed placeholder before publishing.

Template maintained by SarariCorp for consistent policy publishing.
