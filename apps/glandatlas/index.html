Core Commitment

GlandAtlas does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. Your information is used solely to authenticate your account and sync your medical study progress across your devices.

1. Overview

GlandAtlas (com.sarari.glandatlas), developed by SarariCorp, is an educational 3D human gland anatomy, histology, and spaced-repetition study application designed for medical, nursing, and biological sciences scholars.

This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, handled, and protected when you use the GlandAtlas mobile application.

2. Information Collected

GlandAtlas minimizes data collection, collecting only what is strictly necessary to enable account authentication and cross-device cloud synchronization:

Data Category Specific Data Types Collection Mechanism & Storage
Account Identity Google account display name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID) Collected via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) upon user-initiated login.
Study Progress & Activity Experience points (XP), daily study streaks, mastered glands count, and SuperMemo-2 (SM-2) flashcard repetition intervals Stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore to sync study metrics across user devices.
User-Generated Study Notes Private clinical pathology and histology study notes authored by the user Stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore, strictly private to the authenticated user.

The app does not access your device location, camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or financial payment information.

3. How Data Is Used

Your data is collected and used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account Authentication: Verifying your identity and maintaining your authenticated session via Google Sign-In.
  • Cross-Device Progress Synchronization: Syncing your XP, streaks, SM-2 flashcard schedules, and clinical notes across Android devices.
  • App Functionality: Providing uninterrupted offline access with automated cloud synchronization when online.

No Advertising & No Data Monetization: GlandAtlas contains no third-party advertisements, does not run ad-tracking SDKs, and will never sell, lease, or distribute your personal information to marketing brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

GlandAtlas integrates with trusted infrastructure services provided by Google to deliver secure authentication and real-time database capabilities:

  • Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication: Handles authentication tokens and credentials. Review the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Houses encrypted study progress and user-generated notes. Data is governed by Google Cloud's security standards.

5. Data Security

The security of your information is paramount:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the GlandAtlas application and Google Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Firestore Security Rules ensure that only the authenticated user owning a specific account UID can read or write their private study notes and progress data.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You maintain complete control over your data:

  • In-App Note Deletion: You may edit or delete any clinical study notes directly inside the application at any time. Deletions propagate immediately to the cloud.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: You may request full deletion of your user account, cloud profile, and all associated progress records at any time. To request deletion, contact the developer email listed on the Google Play Store listing (support@sararicorp.com) with your registered Google account email. All associated Firestore records will be permanently deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

GlandAtlas is designed as a specialized medical reference and academic study companion for individuals aged 16 and older (target audience: 18+). The application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Educational & Medical Disclaimer

GlandAtlas provides reference information for educational and study purposes only. It is not a diagnostic tool or medical device, and does not provide clinical medical advice. Healthcare professionals and students should exercise independent clinical judgment.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our application or legal obligations. The revised version will always be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date".
