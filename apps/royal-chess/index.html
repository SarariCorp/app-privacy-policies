Royal Chess

100% Offline • Zero Data Collection
Core Privacy Guarantee

Royal Chess is engineered to operate 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or usage telemetry. All gameplay progression, statistics, and preferences remain exclusively on your device.

1. Overview & 100% Offline Operation

Royal Chess (sararicrop.royalchess), developed by SarariCorp, is an offline chess application for Android. We respect your privacy and have engineered Royal Chess so that it operates entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection, user registration, or cloud account.

This Privacy Policy outlines how local data is handled on your device and confirms our zero-collection architecture.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide career progression, game resumption, and customization, Royal Chess stores the following information locally on your device using Android's standard private application storage (Room SQLite database and SharedPreferences):

Data Category Specific Stored Elements Local Storage Mechanism
Gameplay & Career Statistics Offline ELO rating, win/loss/draw counts, difficulty mastery percentages, unlocked achievements, and match history records. Private SQLite database (Android Room) in app-sandboxed local storage.
Saved Games & Learning Progress Active saved chess board positions (FEN), solved daily puzzle IDs, and completed opening lessons. Private SQLite database (Android Room) in app-sandboxed local storage.
Customization & Accessibility Preferences Equipped 3D/2D board and piece theme, 3D camera vertical tilt angle (25°–65°), acoustic sound profile, master volume, haptic vibration preference, and colorblind filter selection. Private Android SharedPreferences on the device.

Strict Local Sandboxing: This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Zero Personal Data Collection or Third-Party Sharing

We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, IP addresses, precise or approximate location data, contacts, photos, or usage telemetry.

The application contains no advertising networks, no third-party analytics trackers, and no background cloud services.

4. Device Permissions (android.permission.VIBRATE)

Royal Chess requests only a single install-time system permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide optional tactile haptic feedback during piece selection, captures, and checkmate. You can disable haptics at any time inside Royal Chess Settings → Multi-Sensory Haptics.

The application does not request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET), camera, microphone, contacts, or shared storage permissions.

5. User Data Control, Modification & Deletion

You have full control over all locally stored data at any time:

  • In-App Reset: Open the Profile tab in Royal Chess and tap Reset Stats to permanently clear your match history and reset your career ELO rating and counters.
  • Android System Storage Clear: You may also clear all local data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Royal Chess → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the application. All local databases and preferences are instantly and permanently deleted by the operating system.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Royal Chess does not collect, store externally, or share any personal information from any user of any age, it complies fully with applicable children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If future updates to Royal Chess introduce new features that affect data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated both on our hosted policy page and inside the application's Settings → Privacy Policy & Data Safety screen prior to release.
